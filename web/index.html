Questions & answers

About the free EUDR forest check.

What is the EUDR cut-off date?

The EU Deforestation Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1115) requires that cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood, and products made from them, placed on the EU market are deforestation-free: produced on land that was not deforested after 31 December 2020.

What do I need to do to comply with the EUDR?

Under Article 9 you collect information on each product: the commodity and quantity, the supplier, the country of production, the geolocation of every plot, and evidence that it is deforestation-free (such as this forest check) and was produced legally. Unless all your products come from countries classified as low risk, where a simplified procedure applies (Article 13), you must also assess the risk (Article 10) and, where it is not negligible, mitigate it (Article 11). Finally, you submit a due diligence statement (DDS) in the EU Information System, on the TRACES platform, before placing the products on the EU market or exporting them.

What does this free EUDR tool check?

For every plot it reports whether there was forest in 2020 according to the JRC Global Forest Cover 2020 map (version 4, 10 m resolution), the forest map published by the European Commission to support the EUDR, together with the forest area in hectares and its share of the plot.

Which file formats can I upload?

GeoJSON, zipped Shapefiles, GeoPackage and KML or KMZ (for example from Google Earth), also inside a ZIP file. Any coordinate system works, as long as the file says which one it uses. Plots can be polygons or points: under the EUDR, a single point is enough for plots smaller than 4 hectares.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The files are read and analysed by your own browser. Your plot locations are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Is the JRC forest map legally binding?

No. The map is an aid for risk assessment, not a legal reference, and it has known errors, for example with agroforestry. Forest in a plot in 2020 does not by itself mean non-compliance, and no forest in the map does not prove compliance.

What should I do if forest is found in my plots?

Take a closer look before drawing conclusions. We verify flagged plots with high-resolution satellite imagery and time series, separate forest from agroforestry and plantations, and document the evidence for your due diligence.

Is it free, and how many plots can I check?

Checking is free and needs no account; a free registration is only asked when you download the results. You can check thousands of plots at once, as long as each one is smaller than 100,000 ha.

Which regions are covered?

The tropics and subtropics, where EUDR commodities mainly come from. Not included: everything north of 60° N (northern Canada, Alaska, Greenland, northern Scandinavia and Russia), and Europe and Asia between 50° and 60° N. Plots there are marked “Outside coverage”.

Forest map: European Commission, Joint Research Centre (JRC), Global Forest Cover 2020 (GFC2020), version 4, EU Observatory on deforestation and forest degradation. Satellite imagery: Mapbox, Maxar; Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (Copernicus Sentinel data).
